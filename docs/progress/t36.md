- Refreshed only the overview, low-angle, Jupiter, Saturn, Earth, and Pluto captures for desktop and iPhone 13 where the appearance intentionally changed.

## Scale and accuracy boundary

- The linear diameter coefficient is a display choice, not a physical distance-to-diameter scale. With fixed JPL elements, the Jupiter apoapsis plus radius remains about 2.06 display units inside Saturn's periapsis minus its ring outer radius.
- Diameters remain sourced from the existing NASA Planetary Fact Sheet body data. The orbit fit uses fixed J2000 elements: JPL approximate elements for the eight planets (Earth-Moon barycentre for Earth) and Pluto SBDB elements normalized to J2000. This is a simplified two-body visualization, not an ephemeris; it omits perturbations and precession. JPL's approximate-position accuracy claim is limited to 1800-2050.
